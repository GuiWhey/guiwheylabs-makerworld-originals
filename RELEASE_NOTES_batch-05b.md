Batch 05b - five new original packages, sliced for the Bambu Lab P1S.

Each zip contains a Bambu Studio project 3MF per plate (Bambu Lab P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard, Generic PETG, Textured PEI, one plate on the 256 mm bed, slice embedded), the STL(s), true-geometry studio renders (4:3 cover first) and listing.txt with a title of 49 characters or fewer, description, tags, real slicer estimates and honest notes.

Nothing in this release was uploaded to MakerWorld. Every part is untested unless its listing says otherwise; slicer numbers are estimates. The 3MF thumbnails and printer id were added by a post-process, not generated by Studio.
